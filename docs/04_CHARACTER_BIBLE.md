# Character bible

**CANON v0.1.** Exactly 25 major characters: Avery + 24 NPCs. Historic Simon and Lydia are not additional active NPCs. Missing schedules, visuals, or occupations remain unspecified rather than invented as canon.

## 01 — Avery Vale

23; player; junior archivist / historical researcher. Returns because Mercer has information about Simon's death. Player-directed, observant, skeptical, quietly persistent, emotionally guarded, curious. Neutral but recognizable silhouette: dark jacket and red scarf. Initially wants the truth about their father; ultimately wants to save Greywick. 1984 phase exposure explains memory persistence. Starts with Mercer's letter.

## 02 — Dr. Elias Mercer

56; electrical engineer and first apparent victim. Precise, stubborn, guilt-ridden, impatient, morally serious. Grey hair, brown coat, round glasses, leather notebook. Wants to stop Meridian, expose the cover-up, and tell Avery about Simon. Knows Avery was exposed in 1984. Default death: 8:11. Knows one half of the safe shutdown procedure. Distinguish his **leather notebook**, **red project ledger**, and **brass M-03 key** as three objects.

## 03 — Arthur Wren

72; retired physicist and catastrophe initiator. Brilliant, polite, obsessive, grieving; rational until Lydia is involved. Thin, long grey coat, cane, wire-frame glasses. Secretly rebuilt Meridian to recover Lydia. Believes her consciousness is trapped in the echoes. Not a cartoon villain.

## 04 — Mayor Eleanor Hale

49; controlled, intelligent, pragmatic, intimidating, protective of Greywick. Dark green suit, pearl earrings, perfect posture. Wants economic survival and family reputation preserved. Knows records were falsified and continues suppressing them. Did not order Mercer killed; initially appears to have done so.

## 05 — Adrian Cole

38; mayor's chief of staff and actual murderer. Composed, loyal, paranoid, calculating, capable of sudden violence. Black overcoat, silver square cufflinks, leather gloves. Wants to protect Eleanor and suppress Meridian records. Hires Daniel, then shoots Mercer during a confrontation. Carries an illegally kept small .32-calibre pistol inherited from his father's belongings; exact action type is unspecified in source.

## 06 — Daniel Cross

24; petty thief; Samuel's nephew. Quick-witted, nervous, charismatic, opportunistic. Brown bomber jacket, messy dark hair, red sneakers. Wants money and to stay out of jail. Paid to steal key and ledger; does not know Adrian will become violent. Not a murderer.

## 07 — Officer Ruth Reed

41; practical, skeptical, observant, dry humor. Police uniform and heavy winter jacket. Wants a quiet town; initially dismisses loop claims. Can prevent murder when given credible, timely information. Prior-loop player knowledge must not automatically become current-loop police proof.

## 08 — Mira Mercer

21; Mercer's daughter. Intelligent, emotional, fiercely independent, impatient. Red sweater, dark shoulder-length hair. Wants her father to leave Greywick. A serious pre-8:00 argument makes her an initial suspect. Knows the location of duplicate research notes.

## 09 — Lena Ortiz

44; café owner. Warm, sharp, gossipy, notices everything. Blue apron, hair tied with a yellow scarf. Sees Daniel unusually close to Mercer and knows Mercer met Felix before the theft.

## 10 — Felix Ward

31; newspaper journalist. Persistent, ambitious, nosy, brave. Tan coat, camera around his neck. Wants the story; Mercer is his anonymous source. Photographs Adrian carrying the ledger near Town Hall at 8:08:30, although a later evidence description places him near the alley. Film contains evidence before Felix understands it; developing and inspecting it need a feasible schedule.

## 11 — Mara Quinn

35; electrical engineer. Extremely competent, blunt, calm. Orange utility jacket, tool belt, dark ponytail. Wants to explain impossible grid behavior. Required for safest shutdown. Understands electrical systems but initially does not know Meridian exists.

## 12 — Owen Briggs

52; maintenance supervisor. Practical, stubborn, funny, suspicious of bureaucracy. Grey coveralls and big moustache. Investigates coolant pressure beneath town. The service room requires Mercer's brass M-03 key; access is a bottleneck.

## 13 — Jonah Price

22; courier; Naomi's son. Friendly, energetic, slightly clumsy. Blue courier jacket and large parcel bag. Delivers Mara's replacement grid synchronizer relay. Default chase-induced collision injures his ankle and prevents delivery.

## 14 — Nora Bell

33; nurse; Clara's older sister. Compassionate, no-nonsense, protective. Treats Jonah after the collision and can give the injury time. Visual unspecified.

## 15 — Ellis Grant

47; mechanic; Mara's former partner, cordial but complicated relationship. Loud, friendly, stubborn, mechanically brilliant. Greasy overalls and welding mask. Starts an arc welder at 8:09:05. Its ordinary load becomes a disturbance under Meridian's draw.

## 16 — Samuel Cross

60; pastor at St. Jude; Daniel's uncle. Calm, thoughtful, perceptive. Knows Daniel has been getting into trouble. Sees Daniel behind church after theft and knows an old underground passage. Visual unspecified.

## 17 — Evelyn Shaw

58; librarian / town archivist; Theo's grandmother. Quiet, meticulous, cautious. Preserved documents Eleanor ordered removed and secretly holds Simon Vale's cassette. Visual unspecified.

## 18 — Theo Shaw

11; Evelyn's grandson and Clara's student. Curious, fearless, talkative. Sees the theft, reports to Ruth, unintentionally initiates the chase/collision chain. Visual unspecified.

## 19 — Vincent Pike

63; locksmith. Grumpy, precise, observant. His company manufactured Meridian locks. Recognizes M-03; can open the service door if convinced. This is an alternate key solution, not a universal lock bypass. Visual unspecified.

## 20 — Grace Hale

27; town clerk; Eleanor's daughter. Soft-spoken, intelligent, uncomfortable with confrontation. Disagrees with suppressing 1984 records. Knows Adrian accessed sealed files and can undermine his alibi. Visual unspecified.

## 21 — Henry Ross

55; depot manager. Impatient, organized, procedural. Logs the parcel's arrival and confirms Jonah should have delivered before 8:10. Visual unspecified.

## 22 — Iris Doyle

29; florist; previously dated Adrian. Warm, anxious, conflict-avoidant. Sees him leave the alley after the shot but initially lies to avoid involvement. Can potentially alert Ruth. Visual unspecified.

## 23 — Naomi Price

46; general-store owner; Jonah's mother. Practical, protective, sharp-tongued. Recovers the abandoned relay parcel after the accident. The critical component then sits in an ordinary shop. Visual unspecified.

## 24 — Clara Bell

36; teacher; Nora's younger sister; Theo's teacher. Patient, disciplined, perceptive. If warned early enough, keeps Theo from witnessing the theft. Without the report, Ruth does not chase Daniel and Jonah can deliver the relay. Source alternates between retrieving Theo and keeping him in school; the action window needs a defined rule. Visual unspecified.

## 25 — Sophie Wren

18; Arthur's granddaughter. Sensitive, intelligent, frustrated with his fixation on the past. Oversized brown cardigan, portable cassette player. Knows Arthur has replayed Lydia's recordings for years. Can help him accept that the echo is not Lydia. Source does not establish that Lydia is Sophie's mother; do not assume that relationship.
