# Story bible

**CANON v0.1 — developer spoilers.** Unresolved chronology and boundary rules are in [the audit](14_DESIGN_AUDIT.md).

## Greywick and Project Meridian

Greywick is a declining industrial/research town of approximately 3,000–5,000 residents. Only 24 NPCs plus Avery are fully simulated. Autumn mornings feel ordinary, nostalgic, peaceful, and slightly wrong.

In the late 1970s, Project Meridian publicly researched experimental energy. Privately, precise electromagnetic resonance revealed information from recent past physical states: **phase echoes**. These are imprints, not conscious continuations or ordinary time travel. Experiments extended echoes from milliseconds to seconds to minutes.

The core researchers were theoretical physicist Arthur Wren, Simon Vale, and electrical engineer Elias Mercer.

## The 1984 incident

Arthur's daughter Lydia worked at the facility. A resonance instability killed Lydia and injured workers. Simon attempted an emergency shutdown. His young child Avery was exposed to an unusual phase pulse and survived; Simon died. Avery is 23 in 1998 and was approximately nine in 1984, subject to birthdays.

Greywick leadership concealed the accident's true nature. The project was officially abandoned. Simon had recognized that convincing echoes of speech, movement, memory, and behavior are recordings, not surviving people. Arthur could not accept Lydia's death and secretly spent fourteen years trying to bring her back.

## The 1998 crisis

Arthur has restored parts of Meridian. The aging town grid can supply one final experiment, with maximum activation planned for 8:15. Mercer discovers the danger and plans an emergency shutdown with Mara Quinn and Owen Briggs.

Mercer also finds proof of the 1984 cover-up. Mayor Eleanor Hale's father participated; Eleanor continues suppressing records to protect her family and what remains of Greywick's economy. She fears exposure and unaffordable lawsuits. This is her belief and motivation, not a claim that secrecy is justified.

Her chief of staff Adrian Cole believes protecting Eleanor means protecting Greywick. He hires Daniel Cross to steal Mercer's red ledger and brass maintenance key. Later, a confrontation escalates and Adrian chooses to shoot Mercer. **Eleanor does not order the murder.** Daniel knows about the theft, not a murder plan.

## Snapshot and reset

At 8:00 Meridian enters stable phase lock and records Greywick's physical state. Instability grows until 8:15. Catastrophic resonance collapse forces the town back into that snapshot instead of producing a conventional explosion first.

Avery's 1984 exposure leaves their neural activity differently phase-coupled. Their body resets but memories do not fully reset. Physical items generally do not persist. A precise treatment of the notebook, boundary crossing, early power cuts, and permanent endings is still needed; see AUD-09 and AUD-10.

## Simon Vale's cassette

Evelyn secretly preserves a recording labeled:

```text
MERIDIAN TEST 14
S. VALE
NOV 1984
```

Source draft dialogue:

> Arthur thinks the field is bringing something back.
>
> It isn't.
>
> We're reading information left in the resonance.
>
> The stronger we make the reconstruction, the more convincing it becomes.
>
> If Lydia speaks to him tomorrow, he will believe her.
>
> But it will not be Lydia.
>
> We need to shut Meridian down.

Avery recognizes Simon's voice. The tape connects their father to Meridian, explains Arthur and Lydia, and gives Avery information to challenge Arthur in later loops.

**Source conflict:** this sounds like Lydia is already dead, whereas the incident summary can be read as placing her and Simon's deaths in the same experiment. Proposed resolution: two linked incidents within November 1984, with the recording between them. Dates remain unchosen; this is not adopted canon.

## Human consequences

Arthur is responsible for starting the catastrophe, but his grief must remain understandable. Sophie provides an emotional connection; Simon's recording alone should not function as a magic password.

Saving the town does not repair Mira and Mercer's relationship, erase the cover-up, restore Simon or Lydia, or make Daniel trustworthy. Adrian's current-loop conduct must determine current-loop consequences; only Avery remembers crimes prevented in later loops.
