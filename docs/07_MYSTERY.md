## Knowledge and persuasion

Player knowledge can unlock a question, predict an event, locate an item, identify a lie, or allow a new argument. NPCs still require a reason to believe Avery in the current loop.

Example: after learning Lydia's name, Avery can ask Arthur about her. After Simon's tape, Avery can say, “The phase echo isn't Lydia.” Arthur can be startled by knowledge and still resist its conclusion. Sophie supplies the emotional context; a knowledge flag alone does not compel belief.

**PROPOSED Ruth route:** Avery identifies a specific future theft or handoff; Ruth observes it; the observed event or a current-loop confession supplies grounds to intervene. Arrest conditions and timing need a separate paper trace. This is a game credibility rule, not a legal-procedure simulation.

## Notebook

Source sections: **People, Timeline, Clues, Questions, Locations, Causality**.

Initially the player may record “8:04 Theo speaks to Ruth,” an unknown interval, and “8:05 Jonah injured.” Later they connect report → pursuit → route change → collision.

**PROPOSED representation:** notebook UI is Avery's reconstructed memory, not a physical book that somehow survives the snapshot. Separate:

- **Observed:** what Avery saw or heard, with time and context.
- **Reported:** an NPC's claim, including possible lies.
- **Hypothesis:** a relationship the player suspects.
- **Confirmed by experiment:** an observed intervention/outcome relationship, without revealing unseen intermediate events.

The notebook must not expose developer-only flags, exact unseen times, or a complete causal graph automatically. Previous-loop knowledge does not preserve a key, cassette, gun, printed photo, or current-loop witness statement.

## Discovery arc

No fixed loop counts or mandatory discovery order:

1. Learn that the morning repeats, Mercer dies, and the catastrophe is strange.
2. Investigate Daniel, Adrian, and the murder evidence.
3. Save Mercer and experience the **false victory**: 8:15 still happens.
4. Investigate relay, coolant, grid, observatory, and Meridian.
5. Discover Simon, Lydia, the accident, Avery's exposure, and the nature of echoes.
6. Orchestrate the morning proactively.

Fast or curious players may discover Meridian first. The game must remain coherent if the murder is prevented before the player ever sees it, or if the player reaches the observatory on the first loop.
